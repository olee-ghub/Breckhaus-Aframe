The house

2,700 sq ftsleeps 8

Experience architectural alpine living beneath sweeping cathedral ceilings and panoramic mountain vistas. This home has three bedrooms, two king suites and a bunk room for four, with a chef’s kitchen, a waterfall island, and a dining table made for gathering.

  • Hot tub & sauna: A private seven-seat hot tub framed by the mountain view, paired with a barrel sauna for four to six. Robes and hot-tub towels are in the house.
  • Deck & views: Mt. Quandary vistas from the deck, the hot tub, and the living room. A fire pit, an outdoor propane fireplace, a gas grill, Adirondack chairs, and dark-sky stargazing.
  • Yoga loft: A quiet loft for yoga, with a mural by a local artist.
  • Play: A games loft with Xbox, foosball, a cocktail arcade, vinyl, surround sound, and board games.
  • Kitchen & coffee: A chef’s kitchen with KitchenAid appliances, a wine fridge, an air fryer, a rice cooker, a Crock-Pot, and a blender. Coffee is Nespresso, drip, or French press, with starter pods, tea, and hot chocolate.
  • Work & laundry: High-speed internet, a dedicated workspace, and a full-size washer and dryer stocked with supplies.

Local guide

Summer & Fall

June – October
  • Mt. Quandary — trailhead about two miles south; summer parking reservation required, or town shuttle to the trailhead.
  • McCullough Gulch — waterfall hike about four miles from the house.
  • Isak Heartstone (the Troll) — short, easy family walk near downtown.
  • Dillon Reservoir — lakeside paths and easy biking.
  • Mohawk Lakes & Continental Falls — alpine lakes and waterfalls.
  • Main Street evenings — dinner twelve minutes north, then back to the fire pit under dark skies.
  • Festivals — Fourth of July on Main Street with the parade, orchestra and a drone show; an arts festival in August; Oktoberfest in late September.

Winter

November – May
  • Nordic Center — groomed cross-country and snowshoe trails.
  • Dog sledding — a ride with local mushers.
  • Sledding — Carter Park, four blocks off Main Street, then back for the hot tub.
  • Ice skating — the town ice arena, indoors or under the outdoor roof.
  • Après — Main Street twelve minutes north, then the sauna under dark skies.
  • Lighting of Breckenridge — early December, with Santas racing down Main Street and the whole town lit up.
  • Ullr Fest & Snow Sculptures — Ullr’s Main Street parade in December, giant snow sculptures in late January.
  • Gondola — ride the free gondola from town to Peak 8.

FAQ

What are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 4:00 PM; check-out by 10:00 AM. Entry is keyless with a digital keypad. Entry code will be sent on arrival day. Early arrivals only when the house is ready; ask and we’ll do what we can.

How far is town and the lifts?

Main Street Breckenridge is a 12-minute drive (6.9 mi) — not walkable. Peak 8 / Breck Connect Gondola parking 7.8 mi; Peak 9 7.5 mi. Keystone and Copper about 30 min; A-Basin about 35 min. Winter traffic can add time.

Are pets allowed?

No pets allowed.

Do I need 4WD or AWD in winter?

Yes — November through May. Plan on 4WD/AWD with winter-rated or M+S tires (min 3/16" tread), or chains when CDOT traction law is on. Neighborhood roads are plowed; driveway cleared after ~4 inches. Pack a scraper and warm layers — trip insurance is wise if you’re flying in.

What’s the drive from Denver (DIA)?

About two hours in clear weather. Add time for I‑70 traffic, mountain storms, and ski-weekend backups — winter drives can run longer. For a 4 PM flight out of Denver, leave the house by late morning.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

High-speed internet, powered for work, play, and everything in between.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 45 days before check-in. 50% refund until 30 days out. Within 30 days, stays are non-refundable.

After you book — and questions before?

After you book. A confirmation email lands right away. Your entry code arrives on arrival day, and check-in is at 4 PM.
